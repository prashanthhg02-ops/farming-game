# Little Acre

A cozy, browser-based farming game. Plant seeds, water your crops, harvest produce, and sell it to grow your wallet.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern web browser. No installation, build step, or server is required.

## How to play

1. Choose a crop seed from the seed pouch.
2. Select **Plant**, then click an empty garden plot.
3. Select **Water** and water your planted crops.
4. Click **End the day** to grow watered crops. Unwatered crops do not grow, but gentle rain waters the garden for that day.
5. Once a crop is ripe, select **Harvest** and click it to add it to your basket.
6. Sell your basket at the village stall to earn coins, then use your coins to buy more seeds.

Carrots take 2 days to grow, tomatoes take 3, and pumpkins take 4. Each crop sells for a different price.

## Saving

Your farm is saved automatically in your browser using local storage. Saved progress stays in that browser on that device.

## Files

- `index.html` — Game page and interface
- `style.css` — Layout, styling, and responsive design
- `game.js` — Farming gameplay, shop, and save data
